Appendix A: Technology

Section A.2: TI-30X IIS Scientific Calculator

The TI-30X IIS scientific calculator can compute summary statistics such as the mean, variance, and standard deviation for one-variable data.

TI-30X IIS scientific calculators include a one-variable statistics mode that computes summary statistics for a list of data values.

One-Variable Statistics: Measures of Center and Spread

To enter data for measures of center and spread:

  1. Enter the statistics mode by pressing 2nd STAT.
  2. Select 1-Variable Statistics.
  3. Press DATA.
  4. Enter each data value and frequency, pressing the down arrow key after each value.

After all data have been entered, press STATVAR. The right and left arrows will scroll through:

  • \(n\): number of observations,
  • \(\bar{x}\): sample mean,
  • \(s_x\): sample standard deviation,
  • \(\sigma_x\): population standard deviation,
  • \(\Sigma X\): sum of the observations,
  • \(\Sigma X^2\): sum of the squared observations.

The sample and population variances are obtained by squaring the sample and population standard deviations.

To exit the statistics mode, press 2nd EXIT STAT, select Y, and press ENTER.

Two-Variable Statistics: Correlation and Linear Regression

To enter data for correlation or linear regression:

  1. Enter the statistics mode by pressing 2nd STAT.
  2. Select 2-Variable Statistics.
  3. Press DATA.
  4. Enter each data value, pressing the down arrow key after each value.

After all data have been entered, press STATVAR. The right and left arrows will scroll through:

  • \(n\): number of observations,
  • \(\bar{x}\): sample mean for \(x\),
  • \(s_x\): sample standard deviation for \(x\),
  • \(\sigma_x\): population standard deviation for \(x\),
  • \(\bar{y}\): sample mean for \(y\),
  • \(s_y\): sample standard deviation for \(y\),
  • \(\sigma_y\): population standard deviation for \(y\),
  • \(\Sigma X\): sum of the observations for \(x\),
  • \(\Sigma X^2\): sum of the squared observations for \(x\),
  • \(\Sigma Y\): sum of the observations for \(y\),
  • \(\Sigma Y^2\): sum of the squared observations for \(y\),
  • \(\Sigma XY\): sum of the products \(xy\),
  • \(a\): slope of regression model,
  • \(b\): \(y\)-intercept of regression model,
  • \(r\): Pearson correlation coefficient.

The calculator's \(x'\) function can be used to estimate \(x\) for a specified \(y\)-value, while the \(y'\) function estimates \(y\) for a specified \(x\)-value. Care must be taken as these are inverse/prediction operations, not automatically equivalent to fitting a separate regression of \(x\) on \(y\).

The sample and population variances are obtained by squaring the sample and population standard deviations.

To exit the statistics mode, press 2nd EXIT STAT, select Y, and press ENTER.
