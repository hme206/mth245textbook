Appendix A: Technology

Section A.5: Key Points

Key Points

  • Learn the concepts before relying on technology.
  • Manual calculations build understanding of statistical formulas and interpretations.
  • Use technology to verify computations and analyze larger data sets.
  • Always interpret statistical output in the context of the problem.
  • Technology performs calculations; people make statistical decisions.
