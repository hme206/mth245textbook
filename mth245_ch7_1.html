Chapter 7: Normal Distribution

Section 7.1: Introduction

Overview

The normal distribution is a fundamental concept in probability and statistics because it provides a useful model for many measurements and random quantities and forms the basis for numerous statistical methods. This chapter introduces the properties of normal and standard normal distributions, including z-scores and probability calculations, and explains how normal distributions are used in practical applications. It also introduces the Central Limit Theorem, which describes the behavior of sample means, and the use of the normal distribution to approximate binomial probabilities.

Learning Objectives

After completing this chapter, you should be able to:

  • Identify the key properties of a normal distribution.
  • Calculate and interpret z-scores.
  • Determine probabilities and percentiles for normal random variables using z-scores.
  • Convert between z-scores and original values.
  • Apply the Empirical Rule to normal distributions.
  • Explain and apply the Central Limit Theorem.
  • Calculate the mean and standard error of a sampling distribution.
  • Recognize when a normal approximation to a binomial distribution is appropriate and apply a continuity correction.
