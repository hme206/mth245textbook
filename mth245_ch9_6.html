Chapter 9: Hypothesis Testing

Section 9.6: Key Formulas

Mean (known \(\sigma\)):

\[z=\frac{\bar{x}-\mu_0}{\sigma/\sqrt{n}}.\]

Mean (unknown \(\sigma\)):

\[t=\frac{\bar{x}-\mu_0}{s/\sqrt{n}},\qquad df=n-1.\]

Population proportion:

\[z=\frac{\hat{p}-p_0}{\sqrt{p_0q_0/n}}.\]

Power:

\[\text{Power}=1-\beta.\]
