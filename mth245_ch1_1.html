Chapter 1: Probability Theory

Section 1.1: Introduction

Overview

This chapter introduces the fundamental concepts and techniques of probability and counting. Students will learn how to describe uncertainty using sample spaces and events, calculate probabilities, analyze compound and conditional events, and distinguish between independent and dependent events. The chapter also introduces Venn diagrams, tree diagrams, permutations, combinations, and counting principles used to solve probability problems in a variety of real-world situations.

Learning Objectives

After completing this chapter, you should be able to:

  • Define random experiments, outcomes, events, and sample spaces.
  • Calculate probabilities using fundamental probability rules.
  • Analyze compound events using unions and intersections.
  • Calculate conditional probabilities.
  • Determine whether events are independent or dependent.
  • Use tree diagrams to analyze multistage experiments.
  • Use counting techniques, permutations, and combinations.
