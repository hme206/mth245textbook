Appendix B: Formulas

Probability Theory

Complementary Events
\(P(A_c)=1-P(A)\)
Multiplication Rule, Independent Events
\(P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)\)
General Multiplication Rule
\(P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B\mid A)=P(B)P(A\mid B)\)
Addition Rule, Mutually Exclusive Events
\(P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)\)
General Addition Rule
\(P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)\)
Permutations
\({}_nP_r=\dfrac{n!}{(n-r)!}\)
Combinations
\({}_nC_r=\dfrac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}\)
Discrete Probability Distribution
\(\mu=\sum xP(x)\), \(\quad\sigma=\sqrt{\sum (x-\mu)^2P(x)}\)
Binomial Distribution
\(P(r)={}_nC_r\,p^rq^{n-r}\)
Binomial Mean
\(\mu=E(x)=np\)
Binomial Standard Deviation
\(\sigma=\sqrt{npq}\)
Correlation
\[r=\frac{n\sum xy-(\sum x)(\sum y)}{\sqrt{n\sum x^2-(\sum x)^2}\,\sqrt{n\sum y^2-(\sum y)^2}}\]
Linear Regression
\[\hat y=b_0+b_1x,\qquad b_1=\frac{n\sum (xy)-(\sum x)(\sum y)}{n\sum x^2-(\sum x)^2},\qquad b_0=\bar{y}-b_1\bar{x}\]

Descriptive Statistics

Population Mean
\(\mu=\dfrac{\sum x}{N}\)
Sample Mean
\(\bar{x}=\dfrac{\sum x}{n}\)
Weighted Sample Mean
\(\bar{x}_w=\dfrac{\sum wx}{\sum w}\)
Grouped Data Sample Mean
\(\bar{x}=\dfrac{\sum xf}{n}\), where \(n=\sum f\)
Population Variance
\[\sigma^2=\frac{\sum(x-\mu)^2}{N}=\frac{\sum x^2}{N}-\mu^2\]
Sample Variance
\[s^2=\frac{\sum(x-\bar{x})^2}{n-1}=\frac{\sum x^2-\frac{\left(\sum x\right)^2}{n}}{n-1}\]
Population Standard Deviation
\[\sigma=\sqrt{\frac{\sum(x-\mu)^2}{N}}=\sqrt{\frac{\sum x^2}{N}-\mu^2}\]
Sample Standard Deviation
\[s=\sqrt{\frac{\sum(x-\bar{x})^2}{n-1}}=\sqrt{\frac{\sum x^2-\frac{(\sum x)^2}{n}}{n-1}}\]
Grouped Data Sample Standard Deviation
\[s=\sqrt{\frac{\sum(x-\bar{x})^2f}{n-1}}=\sqrt{\frac{\sum x^2f-\frac{(\sum xf)^2}{n}}{n-1}}\]
Sample Coefficient of Variation
\(\text{CV}=\dfrac{s}{\bar{x}}\cdot 100\%\)

Inferential Statistics

Normal Distribution
\(x=z\sigma+\mu\) and \(z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\)
\(\bar x\) Distribution
\(\sigma_{\bar x}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}\), \(\quad z=\dfrac{\bar x-\mu}{\sigma / \sqrt{n}}\)
\(\hat p\) Distribution
\(\sigma_{\hat p}=\sqrt{\dfrac{pq}{n}}\)
For \(\mu\) (Mean): use \(z\) when \(\sigma\) is known, \(t\) when \(\sigma\) is unknown
\[\mu=\bar x \pm E,\qquad E=z_c\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}\ \text{ or }\ E=t_c\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}\]\[z=\frac{\bar x-\mu_0}{\sigma / \sqrt{n}}\ \text{ or }\ t=\frac{\bar x-\mu_0}{s / \sqrt{n}}\]
For \(p\) (Proportions)

(\(n\hat{p}\ge5\) and \(n\hat{q}\ge5\) for confidence intervals)
(\(np_0\ge5\) and \(nq_0\ge5\) for hypothesis tests)

\[p=\hat p \pm E,\qquad E=z_{\alpha/2}\sqrt{\frac{\hat p \hat q}{n}},\qquad \hat q=1-\hat p,\qquad z=\frac{\hat p-p_0}{\sqrt{p_0q_0/n}}\]
For \(d\) (matched pairs)
\[\mu_d=\bar{d}\pm E,\qquad E=t_c\frac{s_d}{\sqrt{n}},\qquad t=\frac{\bar{d}-d_0}{s_d/\sqrt{n}}\]\[s_d=\sqrt{\frac{\sum(d-\bar{d})^2}{n-1}}=\sqrt{\frac{n(\sum d^2)-(\sum d)^2}{n(n-1)}}\]
Difference of Means (unmatched pairs): use \(z\) when \(\sigma\) is known, \(t\) when \(\sigma\) is unknown
\[\mu_1-\mu_2=(\bar x_1-\bar x_2)\pm E\]\[E=z_c\sqrt{\frac{\sigma_1^2}{n_1}+\frac{\sigma_2^2}{n_2}}\ \text{ or }\ E=t_c\sqrt{\frac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\frac{s_2^2}{n_2}}\]\[z=\frac{\bar x_1-\bar x_2}{\sqrt{\frac{\sigma_1^2}{n_1}+\frac{\sigma_2^2}{n_2}}}\ \text{ or }\ t=\frac{\bar x_1-\bar x_2}{\sqrt{\frac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\frac{s_2^2}{n_2}}}\]
Difference of Proportions
\[p_1-p_2=(\hat p_1-\hat p_2)\pm E,\qquad E=z_c\sqrt{\frac{\hat p_1 \hat q_1}{n_1}+\frac{\hat p_2 \hat q_2}{n_2}}\]\[z=\frac{\hat p_1-\hat p_2}{\sqrt{\frac{\bar p \bar q}{n_1}+\frac{\bar p \bar q}{n_2}}}\]\[\bar p=\frac{x_1+x_2}{n_1+n_2},\qquad\bar q=1-\bar p,\qquad\hat p_1=\frac{x_1}{n_1},\qquad\hat p_2=\frac{x_2}{n_2}\]
Sample Size for Estimating Means
\[n=\left(\frac{z_c\sigma}{E}\right)^2\]
Sample Size for Estimating Proportions

With a preliminary estimate for \(p\):

\[n=pq\left(\frac{z_c}{E}\right)^2\]

Without a preliminary estimate for \(p\):

\[n=\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{z_c}{E}\right)^2\]
