Interpret Slope

  1. A regression equation relating hours studied \(x\) to exam score \(y\) is \(\hat y=52+6.5x.\) Interpret the slope in context.
  2. A regression equation relating temperature \(x\) (in degrees Fahrenheit) to electricity use \(y\) (in kWh) is \(\hat y=420-8.2x.\) Interpret the slope in context.
  3. A regression equation relating advertising expenditure \(x\) (in thousands of dollars) to monthly sales \(y\) (in thousands of dollars) is \(\hat y=120+4.5x.\) Interpret the slope.
  4. A regression equation relating age of a vehicle \(x\) (in years) to its estimated value \(y\) (in thousands of dollars) is \(\hat y=28-2.1x.\) Interpret the slope.

Interpret Intercept

  1. A regression equation for exam score \(y\) and hours studied \(x\) is \(\hat y=55+5x.\) Interpret the intercept. Is the interpretation meaningful?
  2. A regression equation for a child's weight \(y\) in kilograms and height \(x\) in centimeters is \(\hat y=-42+0.75x.\) Interpret the intercept. Is it meaningful in this context?
  3. A regression equation for monthly sales \(y\) in dollars and advertising expenditure \(x\) in thousands of dollars is \(\hat y=18{,}000+3{,}200x.\) Interpret the intercept.
  4. A regression equation relates the number of years since a company was founded \(x\) to its annual revenue \(y\): \(\hat y=250+40x,\) where revenue is measured in thousands of dollars. What does the intercept represent? Would it necessarily be appropriate to interpret the intercept as a meaningful prediction?

Prediction

  1. A regression equation is \(\hat y=50+4x.\) The observed values of \(x\) range from 2 to 8. Predict \(y\) when \(x=5\). Is this interpolation or extrapolation?
  2. A regression equation is \(\hat y=120-3x.\) The observed values of \(x\) range from 10 to 30.
    1. Predict \(y\) when \(x=22\).
    2. Predict \(y\) when \(x=35\).
    3. Identify which prediction is interpolation and which is extrapolation.
    4. Which prediction should be treated with more caution? Explain.
  3. A regression model is \(\hat y=18+2.5x.\) The observed \(x\) values range from 4 to 12. Predict \(y\) when \(x=8\) and state whether the prediction is interpolation or extrapolation.
  4. A regression model is \(\hat y=90-1.2x.\) The data used to create the model had \(x\) values between 20 and 50. Predict \(y\) at \(x=60\). Explain why this prediction should be interpreted cautiously.

Correlation vs. Causation

  1. An observational study finds a strong positive correlation between the number of hours students spend on social media and their exam scores. Can the researchers conclude that social media use causes higher exam scores? Explain and identify one possible confounding variable.
  2. An observational study finds that cities with more police officers tend to have more reported crimes. Does this correlation prove that having more police officers causes more crime? Give another possible explanation for the association.
  3. A study finds a strong positive correlation between ice cream sales and the number of people swimming at public pools. Does this demonstrate that buying ice cream causes people to swim? Explain and identify a possible third variable.
  4. A researcher observes that students who own laptops tend to have higher grades. The study is observational rather than experimental. Can the researcher conclude that owning a laptop causes higher grades? Explain why correlation alone is insufficient to establish causation.
